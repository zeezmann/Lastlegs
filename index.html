The rules

  • Clubs
  • GroupsTwo groups of eight. Home and away, so 14 games each. Nobody gets a bye and both groups play the same number of games.
  • FormatBoth legs back to back, one sitting. It's all online — nobody's going anywhere. "Home and away" just means who hosts the lobby: first name listed hosts leg one, the other hosts leg two. Both results count separately in the table.
  • Halves6 minutes. A double-header runs about 25 minutes with menus.
  • TimingYou and your opponent agree when. Just don't blow the matchday deadline.
  • HostingWhoever hosts picks the stadium and wears the home kit. That's the only thing home advantage means here.
  • MissedBoth legs lost 3–0. Past the deadline with no game played, that's the result. No appeals.
  • ConnectionDrops in the first two minutes: replay the leg. After that, the scoreline when it dropped is the scoreline. Pull the plug because you're losing and it goes down 3–0. Nobody wants to hear about your wifi.
  • TablePoints, head-to-head, head-to-head GD, overall GD, goals scored — worked out automatically.
  • Level tiesLevel on aggregate after two legs and the player who finished higher in their group goes through. Every round, final included. No penalties, no third leg, no away goals. Finish higher in your group and you carry that with you — that's what the group stage is for. The page works it out itself, so there's nothing to argue about.
  • SeedingCross-group quarters, so group winners can't meet before the final: A1–B4, A3–B2, A2–B3, A4–B1.
  • SpoonBottom four of each group go into the Wooden Spoon — a full eight-team knockout of its own. Still a trophy. Sort of.
