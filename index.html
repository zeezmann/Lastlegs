The rules

  • ClubsDrawn, not picked. Every side in the pot is rated 4 stars. You get what you get and you keep it all tournament — no swaps.
  • GroupsTwo groups of eight. Home and away, so 14 games each. Nobody gets a bye and both groups play the same number of games.
  • FormatBoth legs in one sitting. First leg at your place, second at theirs, back to back. Both count separately in the table.
  • Halves6 minutes. A double-header runs about 25 minutes with menus.
  • TimingYou and your opponent agree when. Just don't blow the matchday deadline.
  • HomeHome player picks the stadium and wears the home kit.
  • MissedBoth legs lost 3–0. Past the deadline with no game played, that's the result. No appeals.
  • TablePoints, head-to-head, head-to-head GD, overall GD, goals scored — worked out automatically.
  • KnockoutAggregate over two legs. No away goals. Level after both legs goes straight to penalties.
  • SeedingCross-group quarters, so group winners can't meet before the final: A1–B4, A3–B2, A2–B3, A4–B1.
  • SpoonBottom four of each group go into the Wooden Spoon — a full eight-team knockout of its own. Still a trophy. Sort of.
